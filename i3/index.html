WHO WE’RE REACHING

Audience personas

Audience descriptions are planning hypotheses—not personal data.
CS
PERSONA 01

College student

18–24 years

Wants practical skills, certificates, internships, and early career momentum; needs an affordable, beginner-friendly starting point.

GoalsBuild a portfolio, gain confidence, become internship-ready.
ChallengesLimited budget, little experience, unsure which skill to choose.
Interests & platformsDigital marketing, analytics, creator content; Instagram, YouTube, Google Search.
Message & recommendation“Start with practical skills you can show.” Lead with beginner guidance, project previews, and transparent course details. Use Reels awareness plus course-intent Search.
RG
PERSONA 02

Recent graduate

21–27 years

Looking for job-ready skills and clearer career opportunities; needs structured training with projects that demonstrate applied learning.

GoalsStrengthen employability, prepare for interviews, build work samples.
ChallengesCompetitive entry-level market, limited practical experience, career uncertainty.
Interests & platformsAnalytics, marketing tools, portfolio projects; Google Search, Instagram, LinkedIn.
Message & recommendation“Learn the workflow, then practise it.” Use project-led course pages, Search campaigns, and an enquiry form with a clear next step.
WP
PERSONA 03

Working professional

25–35 years

Wants to upskill for career advancement and needs flexible learning that fits around existing professional commitments.

GoalsUpdate skills, qualify for new responsibilities, improve career mobility.
ChallengesLimited time, needs credible course fit, balancing learning with work.
Interests & platformsApplied analytics and marketing strategy; Google Search, Instagram, LinkedIn.
Message & recommendation“Flexible learning for your next career move.” Emphasize schedule, syllabus, and outcomes. Use high-intent Search and consented retargeting.
